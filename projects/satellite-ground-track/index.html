Method & Assumptions

Two-body Keplerian propagation: mean anomaly advances linearly with time, Kepler's equation M = E − e·sin(E) is solved by Newton–Raphson for eccentric anomaly, true anomaly and radius follow from standard conic-section relations, and the perifocal-frame position is rotated into the Earth-Centered Inertial frame via the 3-1-3 Euler sequence (Ω, i, ω). Earth-fixed longitude accounts for sidereal rotation (ω⊕ = 7.2921159×10⁻⁵ rad/s). Simplifications: spherical Earth (mean radius 6371 km, not full WGS84), GMST₀ = 0 at epoch, and no J2 oblateness perturbation — real ground tracks (and the ISS's actual ~4.5°/day nodal precession) drift slightly faster westward than this simple model shows. All core physics verified against known values before shipping: ISS period, inclination-bounded latitude range, GEO's 23.934-hour sidereal period, and orbital velocity via vis-viva all check out.
