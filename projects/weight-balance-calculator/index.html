About this tool

Method: moment = weight × arm for each station; CG arm = (Σ moment) / (Σ weight) — the standard procedure from the FAA's Aircraft Weight and Balance Handbook (FAA-H-8083-1). The envelope's forward and aft limit lines are interpolated linearly between the certified chart's breakpoints, exactly how a paper loading chart is read.
  • The station arms, weights, and envelope shown are an illustrative example loosely modeled on a typical 4-seat single-engine trainer — not an exact reproduction of any specific type's certified POH. Real dispatch must use the actual aircraft's own TCDS/POH weight-and-balance data.
  • Fuel is entered as total weight (≈6 lb/US gal for avgas — Jet A is closer to 6.7–6.8 lb/gal; use the correct value for your fuel type).
  • The envelope model is piecewise-linear between chart breakpoints. A real POH envelope can have curved or stepped boundaries this simplification would miss between the given points.
  • Being "in limits" at rest doesn't account for CG shift as fuel burns off in flight — a full flight-planning tool would re-check the envelope at the estimated landing weight too.
